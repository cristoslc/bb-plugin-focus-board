# Purpose

Focus Board gives bb's threads a home you can act from: a kanban board that ranks threads by what needs you, plus a thread pane where you read, reply, and answer agent questions without leaving the board.

## The attention philosophy

The operator's attention is the scarcest resource in the system, and it cannot be split or saved. Focus Board therefore demands only the necessary amount of it, and steers what it takes toward the highest-leverage decision available right now.

Demanding less attention does not mean showing less. Because attention cannot be shared while a decision is being made, the job is to deliver complete, easily parsed context at the single point where it is needed: enough for an informed decision, arranged so the eye lands on the decision first and the supporting context explains it in one pass. Any surface that makes the operator hunt, re-read, or guess is spending attention it did not earn.

## What this means in practice

- The board sorts itself by attention: Pinned, Needs you, Unread, Working, then idle buckets. A card's position is the board's recommendation of where your next decision lives.

- Amber means "the operator is needed". The attention dot, family-box rings, and arrival glints all speak one language, so urgency is scannable without reading anything.

- A card that does not need you should stay quiet: dimmed, settled, out of the amber conversation. Noise in the periphery is billed to the same account as noise in the center.

- An interaction surface (a pending question, its picker, and the history of decisions already made) is the philosophy at its sharpest: it must deliver the full decision context, make the choice itself the highest-contrast thing on screen, and hand back to the conversation without a context switch.

- Surface differences are differences of layout, not of philosophy. Desktop and mobile may use different mechanics (a flyout, a two-pane split, a full-sheet takeover), but each must satisfy the same test: one glanceable decision, complete context, no hunting.
