    ## Focus Board 1.7.12 (2026-11-11)
    
    ### Added
    
    ### Changed
    
    #### Groups and family boxes
    
    - **A group's dashed frame now wraps its done cards in the Done lane.** Marking a member done used to drop its card out of the frame, and a group of one lost its frame everywhere once its only member settled. The frame now follows the group into Done, labelled and still a drop target, while a live family's Done projection card keeps its own slot outside it.
    
    #### The board's lanes
    
    - **A lane's name and caret light up as a chip on hover.** The dropdown chips' faint tint read as nothing on the plain canvas; the pair now takes bb's interactive-state fill and its caret sharpens to full ink, so the lane menu reads as a target before you click it.
    
    ### Fixed
    
    #### Card animations
    
    - **A card that empties its lane finishes its dissolve before the lane closes.** The departing ghost used to vanish the instant its lane left the board, so the card popped instead of easing out. The lane now holds its frame until the dissolve's last tick, then closes with the ghost.
    
    #### Groups and family boxes
    
    - **A Group-filtered board keeps every child of a matched family.** A parent card came up one row short when a child lived in another group. The child now keeps its row inside the card, with its dot and its menu, and is never pulled into its own group's lane.
    
    **Full changelog**: https://github.com/cristoslc/bb-plugin-focus-board/compare/v1.7.11...v1.7.12
