Focus Board — brand icon, gen 4 (catalog idiom)

Start-over. Idioms from bb's plugin catalog (see survey below): bold 2.0-class strokes on a 24 grid, 2–3 forms, no containers, opacity only at large sizes — bb masks plugin icons off alpha coverage, so marks are monochrome currentColor-safe shapes; one asset works for dark and light. Mark the thesis ("the card that needs you"), not the screen. Every candidate lives or dies at 16px.

The catalog's idiom (surveyed)

Real marks from bb's plugin cache, at 16px: compact-nav (window+dots), pokemon (pokeball, 2.0 stroke — the most small-legible mark in the cache), quick-compose (pencil-in-box), quick-chat (bubble+bolt), cascade (the other kanban plugin: artboards of unequal width), clean-my-context (solid eraser hybrid). Note how bold-form + few-elements wins.

A focus ring around one solid landscape card. Two forms; the thesis mark — "this card needs you" — deliberately not a board, because drawing boards is the losing game (see cascade: it does board-composition better, and every board silhouette mushes at 16px).
